About Maverick Welfare Foundation

Driving Purpose-Led Social Transformation From The Grassroots

Incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 as a non-profit company limited by shares, Maverick Welfare Foundation operates with the explicit objective of promoting public welfare, community health, literacy, and sustainable development without distributing profits or dividends.

Evidence-Based Fieldwork

Ground level surveying to accurately pinpoint community vulnerabilities.

CSR & Strategic Alliances

Partnering with corporate CSRs, regional bodies, and academic networks.

All surplus income generated by the foundation is reinvested solely toward advancing our core charter: education, healthcare, women empowerment, ecological conservation, cultural preservation, and disaster rehabilitation.
